The problem

Most Indian households never see where the month's money went. The popular finance apps that promise to help are 50+ MB downloads that demand constant internet, push ads and instant loans, and quietly ship personal financial data to third-party servers.

Budgeting is a habit. A habit tool must be instant, private, and work everywhere — including where the network doesn't.

What it does

  • Log an expense in seconds — amount, category, paid via Cash / UPI / Card
  • Categories for Indian life — Kirana, Petrol, EMI, Bills, Family, Haircut
  • Monthly budgets per category, with clear over-spend warnings
  • Gullak — savings goals: "₹5,000 by Diwali", watch it fill
  • Trends — day-by-day spending, six-month view, month-vs-month
  • Income & balance, recurring entries, search, dark mode
  • Your data is yours — stored on the device, exportable anytime, open source
